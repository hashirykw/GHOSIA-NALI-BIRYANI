The craft

Every degh begins with the shank Bone-in beef goes in low and slow, until the marrow melts into the masala. Basmati is layered over it, the lid is sealed, and the whole thing rests on dum. Then it leaves one small kitchen in Liaquatabad and reaches tables all over Karachi in around ninety minutes.

Inside the degh

What a Karachi degh smells like

  • Shahi JeeraBlack caraway. Smoky, and sharper than everyday cumin.
  • Black PepperWhole corns, for heat that builds slowly.
  • Green CardamomCracked open for a sweet, floral lift.
  • ClovesWarm and deep. A few go a long way.
  • Cinnamon BarkWoody sweetness underneath the chilli.
  • Bay LeafTej patta, tucked between the layers.
  • Dried Red ChilliThe heat Karachi expects from its biryani.
